# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nemo UI a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing
opinions and experiences, accepting constructive feedback, taking
responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or
insulting comments, harassment, publishing another person's private
information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors whose behavior they consider inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the
project maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile. All reports
will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers must respect the reporter's
privacy and security.
